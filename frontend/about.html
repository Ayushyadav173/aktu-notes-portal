About AKTU Notes Portal

AKTU Notes Portal is a free library of study notes for students of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. Notes are organised by year, branch, semester, subject and unit, so you can go from "Second Year, CSE, Semester 3" to Data Structures Unit 1 in a few clicks.

How notes get here

The site owner uploads notes directly. Students can also submit their own. Every student submission waits in a review queue and only appears publicly after the owner approves it.

Using the notes

Notes are shared to help you study. Always cross-check them against the official syllabus and your teachers' material. If you believe a file should not be here, please get in touch.
